# LALITHA Brand Video Specifications

## Primary Hero Video Asset
- **File Location**: `public/videos/hero-jewellery.mp4`
- **Fallback Poster Image**: `public/images/hero-poster.jpg`

## Creative & Visual Direction
1. **Subject**: Traditional Indian High Jewellery / Bridal Haute Parure.
2. **Model**: Indian woman in regal bridal attire adorned with handcrafted 22K/24K gold necklace, polki/kundan choker, ornate chandelier earrings (jhumkas), and maang tikka.
3. **Motion**: Slow-motion cinematic head movement, subtle turn showcasing the craftsmanship, facet reflections, and light refraction of the gold.
4. **Atmosphere**: Deep black luxury backdrop (`#080808`), warm dramatic rim lighting with golden specular highlights.
5. **Mood**: Editorial high-fashion advertisement, reminiscent of international luxury houses (Cartier, Bulgari, Boucheron) fused with royal Indian heritage.

## Technical Specifications
- **Format**: MP4 (H.264 / AVC)
- **Pixel Format**: YUV420p (broadest browser compatibility across iOS Safari, Chrome, Edge, Firefox)
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (Full HD 16:9) or 3840x2160 (4K UHD)
- **Frame Rate**: 30fps or 60fps
- **Audio**: Muted / AAC ambient sound (web video hero uses `autoplay muted loop playsInline`)
- **Bitrate**: 3.5 Mbps – 6 Mbps (optimized for web performance and instantaneous playback)
- **Looping**: Seamless start-to-end loop transition
